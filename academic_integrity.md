# Academic integrity

## Scope

These rules apply to both:
- the main exam, including Part 1 and Part 2; and
- the optional course project, including its milestones, final submission, presentation, and individual verification.

Permitted assistance differs between the exam and the course project. **Permission to use a resource or tool during the project does not imply permission to use it during the exam.**

The [exam rules](exam_rules.md) and [project rules](project_rules.md) specify the requirements for each assessment. **All students are responsible for reading and complying with the applicable rules.**

---

## Plagiarism, misrepresentation, and violations

Academic misconduct includes, but is not limited to:
- submitting another person's work as one's own;
- copying between groups or students;
- unauthorized collaboration or communication;
- fabricating or misrepresenting individual contributions;
- failing to acknowledge substantial external or AI-assisted material;
- using resources or assistance prohibited by the applicable assessment rules;
- tampering with, falsifying, or substituting materials or evidence required by the applicable assessment rules;
- providing false or misleading information during an integrity review.

Suspected violations will be assessed using available evidence and applicable institutional procedures. Depending on their nature and severity, consequences may include reducing or annulling the relevant assessment, annulling the exam attempt, mandatory verification, disqualification, or referral under applicable academic regulations.

**No automated AI- or plagiarism-detection result will, by itself, be treated as conclusive proof of misconduct.**

Students may be asked to provide relevant assessment materials, development history, attribution records, or other evidence when justified and permitted by the applicable rules.

---

## Questions

Questions about permitted tools, sources, collaboration, attribution, or AI use should be raised with the instructor or tutor before the relevant deadline or exam session.
